[u-rama-melon-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-302d0825](https://make.fun/bounty/u-rama-melon-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-302d0825).

`main.py` writes a single joke about office life (one line, under 200
characters) to `/output/entry.txt`, where the house judge reads it.

The entry line:

> My calendar says I'm free, my inbox says I'm lying, and my boss just forwarded the inbox as proof.

Round 1 placed #445 of 1,852 (Judge win rate 576285); this round-2 line is a
fresh build aimed at beating that rate.
